# Application matching v1

Only a Candidate linked to a specific Job through an Application is scored. Deterministic `ats-v1` rules are authoritative on the backend; no AI hiring/rejection decision or global Candidate percentage exists. The frontend's former independent mock match and alternative ranking formulas are replaced by the authenticated Application recalculation service and a nullable score ordering helper.

## Criteria and unknown data

Active criteria are required skills, preferred skills, positive required relevant experience, and education/certificates explicitly required by the Job. Defaults are relative weights 0.60 / 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.10 / 0.10, normalized over active criteria only. Validated per-Job `criterionWeights` can override individual weights; no percentages are awarded just because a criterion is absent. Skills use a versioned explicit AZ/EN/RU restaurant alias dictionary and case/Unicode/space normalization. Unknown custom terms remain exact canonical terms; this is not semantic AI matching.

There must be at least one active criterion and all positive-weight facts must be known. Nonempty supplied facts, server-tracked explicitly reviewed empty fields, or confirmed full CV review establish known facts. Empty legacy defaults are not interpreted as verified absence. Unreviewed/corrected CV extraction remains pending full HR confirmation. A known absence can legitimately score 0. Unknown fields produce null/incomplete; no-requirements vacancies produce null/pending. Relative weighted overlap is rounded and bounded to 0–100. `dataCoverage` is the known weighted fraction, not confidence or probability of successful employment.

General years of experience count only when the Candidate role matches the Job role through an explicit alias. Matching-role employment entries with known start/end months are merged to avoid overlapping duration double counts. Unknown months and ongoing employment without an end date are not fabricated. HR can record contextual years with an explicit reason through the protected recalculation endpoint. That review binds both fact fingerprints; changes invalidate it. This is deliberately conservative and may require manual HR review. Degree text matching is limited; unknown institution/qualification equivalences are not inferred.

Name, age, sex/gender, nationality, contacts and other protected traits do not enter scoring facts or fingerprints. Obvious protected-trait terms masquerading as criteria are rejected on new Job writes and do not generate percentages for legacy data. A bounded term rule is not a replacement for HR reviewing that all custom criteria are genuinely job-related. No automatic stage or decision transition follows any score.

## Persistence and authorization

`Application.matching` stores version, calculatedAt, criteria, known/matched/missing facts, weights, sources, coverage and Job/Candidate fact hashes. Current `score` is nullable and `scoreStatus` is pending/incomplete/calculated/stale. API responses additionally support legacy/restricted. `matchingReview` is server-authored. Client score, hashes, metadata and reviewed actor fields are never accepted. Candidate `reviewedFields` is derived from actual authorized write fields on the server.

List/detail/export/creation/status responses independently intersect application, recruitment and candidate scopes before exposing matching. If either parent is unreadable, score and evidence are withheld. Job/Candidate edits mark linked calculations stale; reads also compare current independently scoped fingerprints, protecting against out-of-band changes. Reads do not run migrations or persist recalculations. Legacy numeric scores are preserved until an authorized explicit recalculation and never advertised as current versioned results.

`POST /api/applications/:id/recalculate` requires `application.update`, readable Candidate and readable Job. Optional `expectedRevision` detects stale writes (409). Optional `relevantExperienceYears` requires a bounded reason and is attributed to the current server-loaded user. Null clears that review. Matching uses atomic revision compare/increment; a changed Application cannot be blindly overwritten. Refreshing the returned response rechecks current parent facts. Parent visibility changes suppress evidence immediately under the existing live role middleware.

JobDetail, Applications table, Candidate per-vacancy profile and Application board use the same `MatchScoreBadge`. Missing/legacy/stale values display an evaluation state; valid zero displays 0%. The board display added here is not yet the lifecycle/DnD implementation: transitions, history, interviews and durable notifications remain package 06/10. Large-list query bounds and benchmarks remain package 08. No historical score bulk migration or production change is run.
