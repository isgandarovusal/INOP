# AI-assisted professional CV review

09C1 provides the minimized, evidence-backed adapter; 09C2 adds independently authorized HTTP/UI review and explicit HR approval. The existing Candidate CRUD/upload contracts are retained. Local mock-backed evidence-review acceptance is tested; real AI E2E remains BLOCKED_EXTERNAL. This is not full original-source extraction or OCR.

## Data boundary

Only a server-loaded Candidate with a registered rules-2/rule-based extracted review and original SHA-256 can supply context. Allowed cv-text evidence fields are skills, languages, certificates, education, role and explicit experience. Raw CV, private file contents/URL/name, Candidate ID/name/email/phone, contact fields, unknown sections and full histories are excluded. Context holds up to 24 identified facts, each bounded to 500 characters. Unsupported/scanned OCR, missing legacy provenance and empty eligible context are explicit AI_NO_REVIEW_CONTEXT=422; existing manual HR review remains available, with unknown values preserved.

Known Candidate names and former extracted-name aliases, email/URL/contact patterns, private-key/token indicators, phone-like digit sequences, control/format characters, markup and common AZ/EN/RU protected criteria exclude an excerpt rather than being forwarded. Unicode compatibility and non-ASCII decimal phone digits are checked. This is conservative filtering of a narrow profession-only source, not a general guarantee that every possible PII/secret or third-party identity can be recognized. Excluded or truncated evidence must be reviewed locally. Whole CVs and contact/name extraction are deliberately not sent. Professional AI review requires separate HR_AI_CV_REVIEW_APPROVED=true plus normal backend provider configuration and approved data-processing/retention terms; the flag alone does not configure an AI provider or prove a lawful production setup. No flag/credential is changed here.

## Evidence and human control

Input is untrusted JSON facts with e1..e24 source references, separated from system instructions. No tools, external URLs or execution of document instructions. Strict output is status, professional proposals and uncertainty. Each proposal includes field/value, same-field evidence IDs, explanation and nullable confidence. Values must literally occur at Unicode token boundaries in each cited excerpt; references to other fields, missing IDs, duplicate facts, invented qualifications, numerical substrings, protected criteria, invalid confidence or decision/extra fields are rejected. Thus 4 years cannot be invented from 14 years, and Go cannot be inferred from mango. Experience must be explicitly numeric 0–80; missing is never zero. Semantic interpretation and provider confidence are still not factual guarantees or calibrated scores.

The adapter cannot discover facts missing from the existing eligible extraction evidence, translate arbitrary qualifications into unsupported new facts, infer dates/years from histories or perform OCR. Rules/manual review handle names, contacts, dates and all existing editors. Broader professional source extraction/OCR is retained in package 10/13, not claimed solved by reviewing the present facts.

Provenance includes schema version/provider/model/mock mode/context hash; a separate local source identity covers original hash, parser version, Candidate revision and millisecond updatedAt for current integration stale guards. No criteria or Candidate fields are persisted by the adapter. HR approval/edit and normal current Candidate read/update authorization are enforced by the integration. Rule evidence remains rule-based rather than being relabeled as an AI-created source.

## Shared limits and evidence

Job and CV calls use the same process-wide two slots, conservative cross-instance MongoDB budget/actor quota, fixed official HTTPS provider endpoint, strict structured response, streamed byte bounds, timeout/cancellation, bounded retry and sanitized counters/errors. No mock provider can be configured at runtime. Missing approval is denied before budget or transport operations.

Backend/tests/hrAiCvReview.test.cjs verifies minimization, Unicode/contact/protected exclusions, empty/OCR/legacy states, bounds, immutable snapshots, identity/revision, same-field literal evidence, false skill/numeric substrings, explicit unknown, decision rejection and synthetic transport/shared capacity. hrAiProvider and hrAiAuthorization preserve the completed Job contracts. These tests do not establish real AI factual quality, provider retention, OCR, storage durability, hosted privacy compliance or a complete original-CV AI extraction flow.

## HTTP/UI integration and source lifecycle

`POST /api/candidates/:id/ai-review` accepts exactly `{expectedHash, expectedRevision}`. Current independent Candidate read and update scopes must both include the resource. The server bounds/verifies the private original SHA before and after the provider, and rechecks current JWT/account/role/department/assignment and Candidate/source identity after awaited operations. Success has structured proposals, minimized evidence, source identity, `reviewScope: professional_evidence_only` and `requiresApproval: true`. No Candidate/score/status mutation. Error status remains 400 invalid context, 401 invalid/revoked auth, 403 denied/revoked grant, 404 unavailable parent/source, 409 changed source/data/authority, 422 no supported evidence, 429 bounded capacity/budget and sanitized provider 5xx.

Authority 409 has distinct `AI_AUTHORIZATION_CHANGED` and hides the old form. `AI_PARENT_UNAVAILABLE` 404 also hides it; `AI_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` 404 is secondary and keeps the authorized manual fields visible. Data/source 409, unsupported/legacy/scanned evidence and provider outage do not discard primary input. Client requires returned source hash/revision to match and rejects malformed proposals before display. HR selects facts, explicitly approves into the editable draft and saves through ordinary authorized Candidate update/CAS. Approving unchanged facts still clears the proposal. Rules evidence is never relabeled AI. Confidence including real zero/null is not a matching score.

No automatic POST on mount/locale change; source/draft/route/auth identity cancels and invalidates pending results. Replacement, reparsing or canceling a replacement prevents use of old-source AI results; save/reopen is required before review of a new source. Unsupported/new/scanned sources remain manual-reviewable. Existing whole-field HR editors stay usable. A later durable draft pipeline is tracked separately, rather than fabricating support here.

`hrAiCvAuthorization.test.cjs` exercises actual isolated HTTP routes and source files for scopes/permission combinations, JWT/assignment/department/account changes, before/after hash/revision and filesystem boundaries; `hrAiCvUI.test.cjs` exercises the real Candidate form for explicit approval/save, source replacement, missing/unknown/zero, late responses, language zero-request, auth/data error separation and AZ/EN/RU copy. `hrAiUI`/`hrAiAuthorization` preserve Job suggestions and test authority 409 and unchanged approval. All provider responses are injected test mocks, clearly labeled; no real provider or real CV is used.
